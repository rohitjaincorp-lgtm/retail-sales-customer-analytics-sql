# Case Study Summary

## Overview

The case study focuses on a retail company experiencing stagnant growth and declining customer engagement.

The company wants to use transaction, customer, and inventory data to improve:

- Product performance decisions
- Inventory management
- Marketing focus
- Customer segmentation
- Customer retention

## Source Tables

### Sales Transaction
Contains transaction ID, customer ID, product ID, quantity purchased, transaction date, and price.

### Customer Profiles
Contains customer ID, age, gender, location, and join date.

### Product Inventory
Contains product ID, product name, category, stock level, and price.

## Analytical Questions Covered

### Data Cleaning
- Cleaning Date
- Fixing Null Values
- Fix Incorrect Prices
- Remove Duplicates

### Sales Analysis
- Total Sales Summary
- Sales Trend
- Growth Rate of Sales

### Product Analysis
- High Sales Products
- Low Sales Products
- Product Categories Performance

### Customer Analysis
- Customer Purchase Frequency
- Customer Segmentation
- Repeat Purchases
- Occasional Customers
- High Purchase Frequency
- Loyalty Indicators
